## Software and models
- [OpenCV](https://opencv.org/) (YuNet face detector, LBF facemark, saliency, CLAHE, GrabCut)
- YuNet model from [opencv_zoo](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo); LBF model from
  [kurnianggoro/GSOC2017](https://github.com/kurnianggoro/GSOC2017)
- numpy, numba, scipy, scikit-image, matplotlib, Gradio, MkDocs Material
